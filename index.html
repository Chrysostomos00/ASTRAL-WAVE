Our Core Services

Expert marine consultancy and technical management tailored to your needs.

  • SIRE 2.0 Readiness – tailored audits, digital inspection checklists, crew training, and corrective action planning.
  • CDI (Chemical Distribution Institute) Preparation – mock inspections, documentation reviews, and practical crew familiarization.
  • RightShip Readiness – gap analysis and action planning to optimize RightShip ratings.
  • TMSA Audits & Implementation – structured support to close compliance gaps and enhance management practices.
  • Flag, Class & Port State Control Support – mock audits and inspection readiness to minimize detention risk.
  • Flag Registration Assistance – advisory and support in choosing and registering under the most suitable Flag State, considering taxation, compliance requirements, operating profile, and trading patterns. We handle the process end-to-end, ensuring smooth registration and certification.
  • ISM/ISPS/MLC Audits & Compliance Support – gap analysis, internal audits, and full support for external audits. Includes creation and improvement of Safety Management Systems (SMS), ship security plans, and labor compliance frameworks.

Value: Reduced risk of detentions, improved vetting scores, and smooth inspections with oil majors, terminals, and authorities.

  • Pre-purchase Inspections – full technical, operational, and compliance assessments.
  • Pre-charter Vetting Inspections – tailored inspections to meet charterer and oil major requirements.
  • Condition & Safety Surveys – objective hull, machinery, and equipment verification.

Value: Independent expertise to safeguard your assets and support commercial decisions.

  • Inspection Assistance – expert attendance during SIRE, CDI, RightShip, PSC, and Class inspections.
  • Port Attendance – liaison with terminals, authorities, and stakeholders to ensure smooth port calls.
  • Cargo Operation & Stowage Plan Assistance – expert supervision and calculation support during loading/discharge operations, stowage plan reviews, and cargo handling audits. We ensure safe, efficient, and compliant cargo operations.
  • Sailing Visits – onboard voyage audits covering navigation, safety culture, cargo operations, and engineering practices.
  • Emergency Support – immediate attendance for incidents, technical troubleshooting, and P&I matters.

Value: Real-time support to Masters and crews, minimizing operational risk and downtime.

  • Drydock & Repair Supervision – cost control, contractor oversight, quality assurance.
  • Newbuilding & Conversion Supervision – design reviews, yard inspections, and commissioning oversight.
  • Technical Superintendent Assistance – on-demand superintendent support on a temporary or per-voyage basis, providing expert oversight for vessel maintenance and operations. This includes troubleshooting machinery failures, liaison with classification societies, coordinating repairs, and guidance through technical inspections.

Value: Optimized lifecycle costs, enhanced asset value, and future-proofed compliance.

  • Competence Audits – bridge team, navigation, and cargo operation assessments.
  • Targeted Training – SIRE 2.0 familiarization, CDI readiness, PSC awareness, safety leadership.
  • Emergency Drill Management – pollution response, fire, grounding, and collision scenarios.
  • Cybersecurity & Risk Management – IMO-compliant assessments and response planning.

Value: Stronger safety culture, fewer incidents, and more effective operations.
